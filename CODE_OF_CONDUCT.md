# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small utility for printing booklets; nothing here is worth being unpleasant over.

## What is expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings about paper.
- Keep criticism about the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that maintainers volunteer their time, and that contributors do too.
- Respect that people work at different speeds and with different levels of
  experience.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour, including about someone's background,
  identity or ability.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad faith argument.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where
someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **hi@nimit.is-a.dev**. Reports are handled privately.
Depending on what happened, the response may be a warning, having a comment
removed, or being blocked from the repository. The maintainers get the final
say, and will try to explain their reasoning.
